Project Overview

Problem Statement & Dataset

Developed as part of the graduate course ET-287 — Processamento de Sinais com Redes Neurais (Signal Processing with Neural Networks) at ITA.

Objective

This project implements a complete machine learning pipeline to predict whether a customer will honor a hotel reservation or cancel it. Although the deployed model is gradient-boosted (LightGBM), the workflow mirrors core practices from neural and statistical learning: representation of heterogeneous inputs, regularization through feature selection, handling class imbalance, hyperparameter search, and experiment tracking.

Dataset Context

Online reservation channels have transformed booking behavior. A substantial share of reservations is called off due to cancellations or no-shows—often driven by changes of plans or scheduling conflicts, and facilitated by flexible cancellation policies that benefit guests but challenge hotel revenue.

Hotel Reservations Classification Dataset (Kaggle) ↗

Target Audience

The primary stakeholders are hotel operators and hospitality platforms (including short-term rental ecosystems such as Airbnb) seeking data-driven tools for operational and commercial decisions.

Connection to ET-287

  • Signal metaphor: Each reservation is a multivariate input vector; preprocessing aligns channels (features) before classification.
  • Decision boundaries: The booster learns non-linear partitions analogous to hidden-layer transformations in neural nets.
  • Generalization: Train/test splits and cross-validated search evaluate out-of-sample performance.
  • MLOps: MLflow logs metrics, parameters, and artifacts for reproducible experimentation.
Applied Value

Why Do We Need This Model?

Three strategic perspectives motivate cancellation modeling in hospitality analytics.

01

Revenue Management

Accurate cancellation forecasts reduce losses from overbooking and under-utilized inventory, enabling dynamic pricing and capacity planning aligned with expected demand.

02

Targeted Marketing

Identifying guest profiles associated with honored stays supports personalized offers and segmentation strategies that match customer expectations and improve conversion.

03

Fraud & Risk Detection

Patterns linked to abusive or high-risk booking behavior can inform screening rules and help operators limit exposure to fraudulent or repeatedly canceling customers.
